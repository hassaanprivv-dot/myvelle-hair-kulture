Know Before You Book

The Rules

01
event_busy

No Same-Day Bookings

Do not book hair or any other appointment same day. Plan ahead — your spot must be reserved in advance.

02
monetization_on

$10 DEPOSIT REQUIRED — NOT INCLUDED IN TOTAL

A non-refundable $10 deposit is required to hold your appointment. This amount is NOT INCLUDED IN YOUR TOTAL — it is separate. There is also a $3 fee if paying the remaining balance via Apple Pay, Cash App, or Zelle.

03
cancel

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations are only accepted 12 hours before your appointment. Cancel after that window and you will be banned from rebooking.

04
bolt

Squeeze-In Fee

Need to squeeze in last-minute? That's +$35 on top of the deposit and service payment. No exceptions.

05

Address will be shared after your booking is confirmed. Services provided in the DeSoto / Dallas area.
